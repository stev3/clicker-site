# Clicker privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Clicker is a remote control app for smart TVs. This policy covers the Clicker app for Android.

**Clicker does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.**

- The app communicates only with televisions on your own local network. It makes no
  connections to the internet.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no accounts.
- To reconnect without asking again, Clicker saves a few details of each TV you pair with:
  its name, its address on your network, and a pairing code issued by the TV. These are kept
  in private storage on your phone. They never leave your phone except when they are sent to
  that same TV, they are not included in Android backups, and they are erased when you
  uninstall the app or clear its data.
- Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, sell or delete on
  request.
